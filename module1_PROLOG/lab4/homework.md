📅 WEEK 4 ASSIGNMENT — “Mini Expert System”
🎯 Learning Goal

Students learn:

    Knowledge base design
    Multi-step reasoning
    Dynamic inference


📝 Assignment: Troubleshooting Advisor

Students build a diagnostic system.

Possible themes:

    Computer problems
    Car problems
    Medical symptoms
    Network troubleshooting

Required components:

    At least 8 symptoms
    At least 5 possible diagnoses
    Rules linking symptoms to diagnoses

Example query:

    ?- diagnose(Problem).

Reflection question: How does PROLOG decide between multiple possible solutions?
