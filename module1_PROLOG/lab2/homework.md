WEEK 2 ASSIGNMENT — “Build a Logic Game”

🎯 Learning Goal

Students learn:

    Constraints
    Logical deduction
    Rule chaining
    Multiple solutions

📝 Assignment: Logic Mystery Generator

Students build a mini detective puzzle.

Required elements:

    At least 4 suspects
    3 locations
    3 possible weapons
    Motives
    Alibis

Required rule:

A rule that determines: 

    guilty(X).

based on logical constraints.

Example ideas:

    Person must have motive
    Must have access to weapon
    Cannot have alibi

Extra credit: Make it so multiple guilty parties can exist depending on evidence.

Reflection question: How does PROLOG eliminate impossible suspects?
