WEEK 1 ASSIGNMENT — “Build a Tiny World”
🎯 Learning Goal

Students understand:

    Facts vs rules
    Queries
    Variables
    How PROLOG searches


Assignment: The Family Universe

Students create a small “human world.”

Required facts:

    At least 8 people
    Parent relationships
    Gender facts

Example structure:

    parent(maria, jose).
    male(jose).
    female(maria).

Required rules they must write:

    grandparent
    sibling
    ancestor

Required queries to demonstrate:

They must show results for:

    “Who are all the grandparents?”
    “Who are siblings?”
    “Who are all ancestors of X?”

Reflection question (important):  What surprised you about how PROLOG found answers?
