📅 WEEK 1 LAB — “Respect the Card”
🎯 Objective

Understand 80-column layout.

Lab Activities
1. Print 80-column templates.
2. Students must:
   
       Number sequence area (1–6)
       Mark column 7
       Highlight Area A (8–11)
       Highlight Area B (12–72)
   
Exercise:

Troubleshoot a broken sample and answer:

    What column error exists?

No compiler. Just analysis.

Reading:

📖 Stern Reading

From Structured COBOL Programming:

      Intro to structured programming
      Overview of COBOL divisions
      Basic program layout

From COBOL/400:

      IBM i environment basics (high level only)

🧪 Homework Lab 1: Skeleton Programs

Students must:

1. Hand-write a complete program skeleton:
   
         IDENTIFICATION DIVISION
         ENVIRONMENT DIVISION
         DATA DIVISION
         PROCEDURE DIVISION
   
2. Label:

         Area A
         Area B
         Sequence area

3. Compile a simple DISPLAY program.


🔁 Repetition Rule

Every lab after this must begin with writing a skeleton from memory.

No copy-paste.
