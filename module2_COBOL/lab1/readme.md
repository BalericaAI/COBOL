📅 WEEK 1 LAB — “Respect the Card”
🎯 Objective

Understand 80-column layout.

Lab Activities
1. Print 80-column templates.
2. Students must:
   
    Number sequence area (1–6)
    Mark column 7
    Highlight Area A (8–11)
    Highlight Area B (12–72)
   
Exercise:

Troubleshoot a broken sample and ask:

    What column error exists?

No compiler. Just analysis.
